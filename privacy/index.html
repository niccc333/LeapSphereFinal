Privacy Policy

Introduction

LeapSphere is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy of our website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines what personal and non-personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when interacting with our pre-launch website.

Information Collected

When you sign up for our waitlist, we collect the email address you provide. Additionally, we may automatically collect non-personal data for website analytics and service improvement, including IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent on our website, referral sources, and user interactions.

How We Use Information

The email address you provide is used exclusively for sending updates about the launch of LeapSphere's AI, providing important announcements, and offering exclusive invitations to our beta program. We do not use your personal information for any purpose beyond those explicitly stated.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We will not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with service providers (Google Analytics, HubSpot, Hotjar) to manage our email list or analyze website traffic, for legal compliance if required by law, or to protect our rights if necessary.

Your Rights

You have several rights regarding the personal information we collect, including:

  • Unsubscribe from communications at any time;
  • Access a copy of your personal data;
  • Request corrections or deletion;
  • Request data portability;
  • Object to the processing of your data under certain conditions.

Data Protection

We take appropriate measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. All personal data is stored on secure servers and safeguarded using industry-standard security protocols.

Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@leapsphere.ai.
