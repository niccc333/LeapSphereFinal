Frequently Asked Questions

What ADAM is, how we work with you, and what to expect.

What is ADAM? Is it software I log into, or is it LeapSphere's team working with AI?

ADAM is a mix of human strategists, algorithms, AI agents, and a dashboard to track progress.

Will AI-written content sound like us, and do we approve it before it's published?

There are brand guardrails in place to ensure every post is cohesive. There will always be a human in the loop unless otherwise stated, and everything gets quality-approved by both the client and LeapSphere.

What results can we expect, and how do you measure them? Is anything guaranteed?

You can expect your website to get recommended to people who need your services, whether they know it or not, through AIEO, GEO, SEO, or social media.

Which CRM do you use? Can you connect to the one we already have (HubSpot, Salesforce…)?

We can connect to any CRM you already use. We also provide a proprietary CRM that has no learning curve and all the features of the big names.

Do you work with clients outside Quebec or Canada, and in French as well as English?

We currently work in English, French, and Spanish.

Do you work with our competitors?

We only work with highly specialized firms in their niches. If we work with you, we won't work with your direct competitors.

Do we keep ownership of the content and the data if we leave?

Yes, everything gets returned as is, along with the recommended strategies and all data collected until the end of the contract.

What happens on the discovery call, and is it free?

The discovery call is free, and it's where we gauge how good the fit is between LeapSphere and your business.
