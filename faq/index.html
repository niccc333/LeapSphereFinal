Frequently Asked Questions

What ADAM is, how we work with you, and what to expect.

What is Adam? Is it software I log into, or a team?

You work with the LeapSphere team, and Adam is the engine behind your marketing department. Our strategists set the direction for your firm, and Adam carries it out every day. So it’s not a tool you have to operate, and not a traditional agency.

Will the content sound like us, and do we approve it first?

We start with an in-depth study of how your firm speaks, then set firm brand rules that every piece of content must follow. That keeps the writing on brand every time, often more consistently than a human writer can. Our team reviews everything before it goes out, and you approve the plan before anything is written.

What results can we expect? Is anything guaranteed?

We don’t guarantee exact results, because anyone who does is typically promising more than marketing can honestly deliver. What we do is find people looking for a firm like yours right now and bring them to your site. You see your rankings, results, and lead records throughout, so you’re always in the loop and in control.

Which CRM do you use? Can you connect to ours?

Yes, we can connect to the one you already use, such as HubSpot or Salesforce. Or use the one that comes with Adam. Most CRMs typically rely on someone updating them by hand, while ours is always in context, connected to your marketing, and runs itself.

What if we already have a marketing team?

Then they get a department behind them. Adam handles the daily execution, such as content, SEO, social, email, and lead follow-up, so your team can focus on strategy, relationships, and the work only people can do. They stay in control, and they can do far more than before.

Do you work with clients outside Quebec, and in French as well as English?

Yes to both. We write natively in English and French, so nothing reads like a translation. Spanish is coming soon.

Do you work with our competitors?

Everything we build is made for your firm alone: your strategy, your voice, your market position. Two firms in the same field never get the same plan, and your data stays yours. We’re also careful about who we take on in the same niche.

Do we keep our content and data if we leave?

Yes. You own everything: the content, your rankings and results, and your lead records.

What happens on the discovery call, and is it free?

It’s free. We learn about your firm and goals, and we both see whether we’re a good fit.
