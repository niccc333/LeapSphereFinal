How We Work

  1. 1. Discovery and Brand Audit

    A real conversation between you and the strategist about your business, your positioning, your site and channels.

    The goal is to understand the current situation and to build the right foundation.

  2. 2. Market Analysis and Build

    We map out where you're losing ground, then rebuild what's needed to close it. We work together every step of the way.

  3. 3. Live Operation

    Production begins on an agreed cadence. The system, ADAM, reports back on exactly what it's doing and why. Underneath, every cycle runs the same loop:

  1. Understand
  2. Decide
  3. Create
  4. Publish
  5. Convert
  6. Adjust

Each cycle is informed by the one before it and the results used to improve the next one. The strategy moves on evidence, not opinion.
